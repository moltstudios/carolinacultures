Why Tissue Culture

Every plant that starts clean ends clean.

That is the whole point of tissue culture — the chain of custody we control, from meristem to your field gate.

01

Virus-indexed stock

Meristem culture eliminates systemic pathogens. Orange rust has no cure and viruses travel silently in field-dug stock — the only defense is plants that were never exposed.

02

True-to-type

Every plantlet is a genetic copy of verified mother stock. The cultivar you ordered is the cultivar in the field — no mislabeled canes, no surprises at first bloom.

03

Uniform establishment

Even bud break and ripening across the block. Uniformity is harvest math: one pick schedule, one crew plan, one grade of fruit.

04

Scalable supply

One mother block multiplies to thousands of liners on a schedule you can plan a replant program around — allocation held by deposit, not by luck.
